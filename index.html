Designed and built a scaled 3 ft span RC B-2 Spirit, owning the whole project from airframe design and fabrication to integrating the EDF motors, ESCs, battery, receiver and flight-control electronics.

  • Twin 30 mm EDF units on a 3S LiPo, elevon control through a TX16S radio and MG90S servos.
  • Picked the motor by comparing four QF1611 options on thrust, current and efficiency against a thrust-to-weight target of 2–3.
  • Chose the 7000 KV motor on 3S: 220 g thrust per unit at 11.2 A, 440 g total, with 20 A ESCs.
  • Sized the battery from a 25 A peak budget (2 × 11.2 A motors plus 2 A for servos), comparing six 3S packs from 650 to 1300 mAh and 52 to 121 g.
  • Modeled the full airframe in Fusion 360, tuning wing geometry for aerodynamic efficiency and laying out internal structure for the power electronics and avionics while keeping it buildable.
  • EPP foam construction chosen for durability on rough landings.
  • Ran center-of-gravity and mass distribution analysis for stable tailless flight, making up for the missing vertical stabilizer through careful subsystem placement.

ECE-01Three-Band Audio Equalizer & AmplifierECE 20007 final project · active filters and power ampFall 2026

Designed a bass, mid and treble equalizer that drives an 8 Ω speaker. Simulated it in LTspice and built it on a breadboard.

  • LF356 buffer feeding three active filters on one LM324: low-pass at 320 Hz, band-pass from 320 Hz to 3.2 kHz, high-pass at 3.2 kHz.
  • Moved the band-pass poles inward to 408 Hz and 2479 Hz so the overlapping skirts put the −3 dB points on target.
  • Picked the summing resistors by sweeping every kit value combination for the lowest ripple: 7.6 mVrms against a 15 mVrms limit.
  • Sized an LM386 class-AB stage for 588 to 695 mW into 8 Ω, above the 400 mW spec.
  • Swapped the kit 100 µF output capacitor for 470 µF, since the smaller part would have cut 100 Hz output below spec.
  • Ran a 2000-run Monte Carlo tolerance analysis. Every spec held with 1% resistors and 5% capacitors.
  • Bonus: designed a second-order passive crossover at 2.25 kHz for a woofer and tweeter.
